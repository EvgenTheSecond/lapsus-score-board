# Lapsus — Rules for 2 Players

> These are the two-player rules and house rules we used while playing, not a verified copy of the official rulebook.

## 1. Setup

- **Players:** 2
- **Roles:** Speaker and Translator (switch roles each round)
- **Materials:** Topic cards, syllable cards, and a 1-minute timer
- **Objective:** Create a word that helps the Translator identify the correct topic.

## 2. Roles

### Speaker

- Receives **8 syllable cards** and **1 topic card**.
- Looks at the topic without showing it to the Translator.
- Has **60 seconds** to create a word associated with the topic.
- Cannot speak or give verbal hints.

### Translator

- Does not know the Speaker's topic.
- May watch the Speaker assemble the word.
- Must select the correct topic from **3 topic cards**.

## 3. Round Sequence

1. Give the Speaker 8 syllable cards and 1 topic card.
2. The Translator draws 2 additional topic cards without looking at them.
3. Start the 60-second timer.
4. The Speaker assembles a word using syllable cards.
5. When time runs out, the Speaker stops modifying the word.
6. Mix the Speaker's topic card with the 2 additional topic cards.
7. Reveal all 3 topic cards to the Translator.
8. The Translator chooses the topic they think matches the word.
9. Reveal the correct topic and award points.
10. Switch roles for the next round.

## 4. Word Construction

- The Speaker may use fewer than 8 syllable cards.
- Cards can be rearranged during the 60 seconds.
- Cards may overlap to hide or combine letters.
- Invented words and creative associations are allowed.
- The Speaker cannot reveal the topic or give verbal hints.

## 5. Time Limits

| Action | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Constructing a word | 60 seconds |
| Guessing the topic | No time limit |

## 6. Scoring (2-Player House Rules)

| Result | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Translator guesses correctly | Translator earns 1 point |
| Translator guesses incorrectly | No points |

**Optional cooperative variation:** If both players agree, award 1 point to each player for a successful round. Note that this produces tied individual scores, so it works better as a shared team score.

## 7. Winning

- Agree on a number of rounds (for example, 10).
- Alternate roles each round so both players have equal chances to guess.
- The player with the most points wins.
- If tied, play extra rounds in pairs, giving each player one turn as Translator, until the tie is broken.
