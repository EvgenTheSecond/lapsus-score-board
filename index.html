A quick rules refresher+

Build a word

The Speaker gets 8 syllable cards and a secret topic. Start the minute and build a word without speaking. Overlap and rearrange cards as you like.

Make a guess

When time is up, stop building. Mix the secret topic with 2 other topic cards. The Translator chooses from all 3, with no time limit.

Score & switch

A correct guess earns the Translator 1 point. Otherwise, no points. Switch roles each round. Agree on an even number of rounds; settle ties with extra rounds in pairs.
